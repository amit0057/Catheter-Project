# Catheter-Project

My team and I collaborated to develop a convolutional neural network (CNN) to analyses X-ray images and classify catheter types, enabling identification of catheter and their placement which is normal vs abnormal. This case study demonstrated potential application in hospital settings, particularly emergency departments, to support early detection of high-risk misplacements.

# Responsibilities:
  ▪	Collaborated with team members to design and develop a CNN.
  ▪	Researching and experimenting architectures such as ResNet and DenseNet.
  ▪	Performed data cleaning and addressed significant class imbalance within the dataset. 
  ▪	Communicated findings clearly to stakeholders and provided recommendations for future.
